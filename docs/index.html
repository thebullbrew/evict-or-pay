Why the answer almost always lands the same way

An eviction doesn't get you the back rent — a judgment against someone who can't pay rent is a souvenir, not an asset. What it buys you is months of dead unit, legal bills, and a trashed apartment. Cash-for-keys buys you the only things that matter: the keys, a clean unit, and a paying tenant back in fast.

The exception is the principle landlord — the one who'd rather burn $5,000 teaching a lesson than $2,000 solving a problem. That's a hobby, not a business.
